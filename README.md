# Spotify-Analysis
In today’s fast-evolving digital music landscape, understanding behavior is critical for streaming platforms, record labels, and artists alike. The Spotify Albums Data Analysis project examines user engagement across albums, tracks, and artists to surface actionable intelligence on music consumption trends, changing audience preferences over time
Strategic Goals & Features

Listener Behavior Tracking: Analyze consumption habits over time to pinpoint emerging music trends and shifts in audience engagement.

Content Performance Evaluation: Benchmark top-performing albums, individual tracks, and artists to determine what drives maximum replay value and user retention.

Interactive Visualization: Build a dynamic, user-friendly dashboard that presents complex streaming metrics through clear, actionable visual components.

Business-Driven Insights: Empower stakeholders—from product teams to music marketers—to make data-backed decisions regarding playlist curation, promotional campaigns, and artist partnerships.
